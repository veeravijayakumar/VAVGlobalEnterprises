About
Home → About Us

Inner wear is the first layer of every day.

Born in 2020. Crafted for Women. Made to Go Further


Since 2020, V has been creating women's innerwear with one simple belief: every woman deserves comfort she can trust.


What began as a commitment to quality and everyday comfort has grown beyond borders, with our products reaching markets across the UAE and Australia.
With our own production capabilities, we stay closely involved in every stage of our products—from the selection of fabrics to the fit, finish and final quality.
We believe in experiencing what we create. products are personally used and evaluated by us, allowing us to understand comfort from a woman's perspective and continuously improve our products.

Our core focus is high-quality cotton women's innerwear, thoughtfully made to be soft, breathable, comfortable and suitable for everyday life.
At V, we believe quality should feel accessible. Our production capabilities allow us to maintain high standards while offering our products at an affordable price.

2020. From our production floor to women across the world.

Our Journey

Fourteen years, five milestones.

2020
Founded in Tirupur

VAV Global Enterprises starts with six knitting machines and a single cutting table in a 4,000 sq.ft. unit. First order: 12,000 cotton vests for a Tamil Nadu retail chain.

2021
Premium Reserve Line

Launched the Premium Reserve line with Lenzing micro-modal at 220 gsm. First Indian inner-wear brand to use bonded waistband construction at this price point.

2022
40,000 sq.ft. Facility

Moved into a single-building facility — knitting, dyeing, cutting, sewing, finishing, packing under one roof. Same team, same standard, ten times the capacity.

2024
1.4M Pieces / Year

Now producing 2.4 million pieces annually with 82 wholesale partners across India. Active Base line launched with recycled poly-spandex for sport and long-shift wear.
